## Submission Copy

**Name:** ResumeGen

**Tagline:** Free AI resume generator and ATS resume maker for tailored job applications.

**Short description:** ResumeGen helps job seekers upload or enter a resume, paste a job description, improve ATS keyword match, and download a professional resume template as PDF or DOC.

**Categories:** AI tool, Resume builder, Productivity, Recruiting, Career, Writing assistant

**Best links to use:**

- Homepage: `https://www.resumegen.pro/`

- Resume builder pages: `https://www.resumegen.pro/free-resume-builder`

## Tracking

Add UTM tags per submission so Search Console and analytics can show which directories actually send traffic.

Example:

```text
https://www.resumegen.pro/ai-resume-generator?utm_source=futuretools&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=backlinks
```

For SEO listings, submit the clean canonical URL when the directory stores a permanent profile link, and use UTM links only when they allow campaign URLs without replacing the canonical product URL.
